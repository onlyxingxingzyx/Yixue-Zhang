A little about me

✧

Hello! I’m Yixue, a graduate student at the College of Engineering, Peking University.

My research explores how intelligent agents can translate visual and language understanding into reliable physical actions. I am particularly interested in VLA pre-training, agentic task generation, multimodal representations, and reinforcement learning for robotic manipulation.

Previously, I worked on embodied intelligence algorithms at the Beijing Innovation Center of Humanoid Robotics and language-model data algorithms at Microsoft TCC.

Research interests ✦

  • Vision-Language-Action Models
  • Robot Learning & Manipulation
  • Agentic Task Generation
  • Reinforcement Learning
  • Multimodal Representation Learning
